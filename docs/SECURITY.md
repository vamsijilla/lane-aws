# Security and fair treatment

## Fair treatment

Lane underwrites the risk, not the person. Indicators of legal presence or immigration status are
never underwriting signals:

- License validity comes from the state motor vehicle record (MVR) only (assumption A10). A
  license card's designation (for example limited-term, non-domiciled or temporary visitor) is
  not stored, shown, scored or passed to the analyst model.
- No rule, finding or agent request may rest on citizenship, national origin, legal presence or
  the type of identity document a driver holds.
- The analyst's brief carries resolved facts only; a finding must cite a claim or rule id, so a
  finding with no underwriting basis in the guideline has nothing to cite.

When a document shows such an indicator, Lane reads the facts the guideline needs (license class,
status on the MVR, violations, dates) and ignores the rest.
