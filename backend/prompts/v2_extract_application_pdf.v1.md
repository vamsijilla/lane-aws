Document type: a full commercial auto application printed as a PDF or image. Fill the fields that the form shows and leave the rest null; never infer a value the form does not print.
- garaging_address is the full garaging (parking) address line; garaging_state is its state.
- signature is the signer's name or signature mark beside a signature label; null when the signature line is blank.
- questions holds every yes or no operational question with its marked answer, including questions printed inline as "Question: yes".
- drivers holds every listed driver. prior_insurance holds one record per line and term, with the line (for example Auto liability or Cargo) in coverage.
- radius and commodities hold one record per band or commodity with its percent.
