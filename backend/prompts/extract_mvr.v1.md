Document type: motor vehicle record (MVR) for one driver, possibly continued over several pages.
- events: one entry per violation, conviction, accident, suspension or failure to appear in the record tables, with points and at-fault exactly as printed. An empty table ("NONE TO REPORT") means no events.
- notes: dated entries from the miscellaneous or state data section that matter for a commercial driver: CDL downgrade, medical certificate, status, clearance, renewal, transfer, duplicate license issued, point credits. Skip pure address-change or mailing notices.
- license_type: commercial or personal / non-commercial, as the license section states.
