Document type: motor vehicle record (MVR) for one driver, possibly continued over several pages.
- events: one record per violation, conviction, accident, suspension or failure to appear in the record tables, with points exactly as printed, and one record per CDL action stated in the record (CDL issued, renewed, downgraded or suspended, with kind cdl_issued, cdl_renewed, cdl_downgraded or cdl_suspended). An empty table ("NONE TO REPORT") means no events.
- notes: other dated entries from the miscellaneous or state data section (clearance notices, point credits, duplicates issued). Skip pure address-change or mailing notices.
- license_type: commercial or personal / non-commercial, as the license section states.
