Document type: a quick quote or indication request form.
- radius and commodities: one entry per printed bucket or commodity with its percentage and value.
- questions: every numbered yes or no question with the marked answer.
- drivers: only rows that hold a driver; a row that says a list is attached goes to no row.
- prior_insurance: one entry per prior policy row.
- answers to questions printed outside the numbered list (renewal or new, previously submitted, physical damage, cargo, reefer) go to their own fields; blank means null.
