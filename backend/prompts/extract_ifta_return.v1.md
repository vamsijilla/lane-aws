Document type: an IFTA quarterly fuel tax return or filing record, usually a summary page followed by jurisdiction tables that continue across pages.
- rows: one entry per jurisdiction line on every page, including surcharge lines (put "Surcharge" in surcharge), in printed order. Negative amounts keep their minus sign.
- totals: the bold totals line under the last jurisdiction row.
- subtotal_due, penalty, interest, total_due come from the summary box.
